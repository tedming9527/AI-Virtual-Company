# Company skill governance

Read the company-root policy at `skills/SKILL_ASSET_POLICY.md` (resolved via $AI_VIRTUAL_COMPANY_ROOT; the relative `../` path works only inside the source tree, not in standalone adapters).（2026-09-24 审计修复）

Choose a skill only if it materially improves the routed task’s outcome, reliability, safety, or repeatability. Keep the selection minimal. Record considered and used skills with a short rationale in the work brief. Do not treat any company skill as automatically loaded by a project binding.

For a new reusable skill or knowledge artifact, create its canonical form in the company root, register it in the company-root `ASSET_REGISTRY.md` (company-wide registry); `skills/ASSET_REGISTRY.md` is the skill-subset registry and must be kept in sync. Assign a steward, then publish a platform adapter only when needed.（2026-09-24 审计修复）
